# Features

## Customer-facing experience

YuHome was developed to provide a structured digital experience for short-stay accommodation discovery and reservation.

Key areas include:

- property presentation;
- accommodation details and images;
- availability information;
- reservation workflows;
- responsive navigation;
- Portuguese and English experience.

## Reservation management

The application centralizes reservation-related operations instead of relying exclusively on disconnected manual communication.

The system supports the organization of:

- booking information;
- booking periods;
- customer information;
- property availability;
- reservation status and operational follow-up.

## Property management

Administrative workflows support the management of the content required to present accommodation online, including property information and media.

## Customer management

Customer information connected to reservation workflows can be managed centrally by authorized users.

## Payment management

Payment-related information can be associated with the operational booking process and managed through the application's administrative workflows.

## Administration

The administrative side of the platform supports the operational management of the system, including relevant reservation, property, customer and reporting workflows.

## Reporting

Operational information can be consolidated into administrative views and reports to improve visibility over platform activity.

## Responsive design

The interface is designed for use across desktop and mobile devices.

## Multilingual experience

The public platform supports Portuguese and English, improving accessibility for different customer profiles.
